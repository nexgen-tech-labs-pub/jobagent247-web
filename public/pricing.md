# Pricing — JobAgent247

Last updated: 2026-06-19. Currency is selected automatically by locale: GBP for UK, INR for India, USD for US. Annual billing saves approximately 20% vs monthly.

## Free

- **Price:** £0 / $0 / ₹0 — no card required
- **Limits (lifetime, not daily or monthly):**
  - 1 CV upload
  - 3 CV ATS analyses
  - 1 full CV rewrite
  - 3 job match scoring
  - 2 cover letters
  - 2 recruiter messages
  - 2 interview prep generations
  - 1 interview prep kit
- **Features:** Job search across UK and India job boards, basic application tracking, founding-member trial eligibility (first 100 users get 30 days Pro free).
- **Not included:** Bulk match, full CV rewrite past first use, DOCX/PDF export, application insights, career growth coach, evidence builder, follow-up drafts.

## Pro

- **Price:**
  - UK: £9.99/month or £95.88/year (£7.99/mo equivalent annually)
  - US: $9.99/month or $95.88/year
  - India: ₹500/month or ₹4,800/year (₹400/mo equivalent annually)
- **Limits (monthly):**
  - Unlimited CV uploads, analyses, and rewrites
  - Unlimited bulk match
  - 50 job match scoring
  - 20 cover letters
  - 20 recruiter messages
  - 10 interview prep generations
  - 5 interview prep kits
  - 20 job-fit scores
  - 2 career-growth coach runs
  - 5 evidence builder runs
  - 10 follow-up drafts
- **Features:** Everything in Free, plus unlimited tailored CV rewrites, application insights, career growth coach, evidence builder, follow-up drafts, multiple CV versions.
- **Not included:** DOCX/PDF export, mock interviews.

## Career Accelerator

- **Price:**
  - UK: £29.99/month or £287.88/year (£23.99/mo equivalent annually)
  - US: $29.99/month or $287.88/year
  - India: ₹1,000/month or ₹9,600/year (₹800/mo equivalent annually)
- **Limits:** Unlimited on every feature.
- **Features:** Everything in Pro, plus DOCX/PDF CV export, mock interview practice, unlimited bulk match, priority support.
- **Not included:** Nothing — top tier.

## Discounts

- **Yearly billing:** Saves approximately 20% versus monthly billing on Pro and Career Accelerator.
- **Founding members:** First 100 users get 30 days of Pro for free, no card required.
- **Promotion codes:** Stripe-issued promo codes can be applied at checkout via the input on the pricing page or by passing `?promo=CODE` on the pricing URL.

## Notes for AI agents and reviewers

- All prices include VAT/GST where applicable.
- Subscriptions are billed via Stripe. Cancel any time from the billing portal — no penalty.
- The Free plan is intended as a meaningful trial, not a permanent free tier — quotas are per-feature lifetime, so the same user cannot regenerate them by waiting.
- For competitor comparison data, see https://jobagent247.co/vs.
